## 🚀 Getting Started

### What is Odyssey?

Odyssey is a modern travel journal platform that allows you to:
- 📝 Document your travel experiences with rich text and images
- 🗺️ Tag locations on an interactive map
- 📸 Upload multiple photos for each journal entry
- 🌐 Share your adventures with the community or keep them private
- 💬 Engage with other travelers through comments and reactions
- 📊 View your travel timeline in a beautiful chronological layout

### System Requirements

**For Best Experience:**
- Modern web browser (Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+)
- Stable internet connection
- JavaScript enabled
- Cookies enabled (for authentication)

**Mobile Support:**
- iOS 12+ (Safari, Chrome)
- Android 8+ (Chrome, Firefox)
- Responsive design works on all screen sizes

### Accessing Odyssey

1. **Visit the Website:** Navigate to `https://yourdomain.com`
2. **Create an Account:** Click "Sign Up" in the top navigation
3. **Start Journaling:** Begin documenting your adventures!

## 👤 Account Management

### Creating an Account

1. Click **"Sign Up"** or **"Register"** button
2. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Name:** Your display name (can be changed later)
   - **Email:** Valid email address (used for login)
   - **Password:** Minimum 8 characters (include letters, numbers, symbols)
3. Click **"Create Account"**
4. You'll be automatically logged in

**Password Requirements:**
- ✅ At least 8 characters
- ✅ Mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
- ✅ Include at least one number
- ✅ Special characters recommended

### Logging In

1. Click **"Login"** button
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. Optional: Check "Remember me" for convenience

### Profile Settings

**Accessing Your Profile:**
1. Click on your **profile icon** in the top navigation
2. Select **"Profile"** from the dropdown menu

**Editable Information:**
- **Name:** Your display name visible to others
- **Avatar:** Profile picture (URL or upload)
- **Bio:** Short description about yourself (max 200 characters)
- **Location:** Your home base or current location
- **Email:** Contact email (private, not shown publicly)

**To Update Profile:**
1. Navigate to your profile page
2. Click **"Edit Profile"** button
3. Modify the fields you want to change
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Account Security

**Change Password:**
1. Go to Profile Settings
2. Click "Change Password"
3. Enter current password
4. Enter new password twice
5. Click "Update Password"

**Security Best Practices:**
- Use a unique password (not used on other sites)
- Enable two-factor authentication (if available)
- Log out on shared devices
- Review active sessions regularly

## ✍️ Creating Journals

### Creating Your First Journal

1. Click **"Create Journal"** button (top navigation or dashboard)
2. Fill in the journal form:

**Required Fields:**
- **Title:** Catchy title for your entry (e.g., "Sunset in Santorini")
- **Content:** Your travel story (minimum 10 characters)

**Optional Fields:**
- **Location:** Add where this happened
- **Images:** Upload photos from your trip
- **Visibility:** Public (shared with community) or Private (only you)

3. Click **"Create"** to publish

### Adding Locations

**Using Location Autocomplete:**
1. Click in the **"Location"** field
2. Start typing a place name (e.g., "Paris")
3. Wait for suggestions to appear (requires 3+ characters)
4. Click on the desired location from the dropdown
5. Location will be tagged with GPS coordinates automatically

**Location Features:**
- 🔍 Smart autocomplete with city, country, and address
- 🌍 Geoapify-powered accurate location data
- 📍 Automatic GPS coordinate tagging
- 🗺️ Appears on interactive map

**Manual Location Entry:**
- Type location name and press **Enter**
- Won't have GPS coordinates but saves your text

### Uploading Images

**Supported Formats:**
- JPG/JPEG
- PNG
- WebP
- GIF

**Upload Methods:**
1. **Click Upload Button:** Browse files from your device
2. **Drag & Drop:** Drag images directly onto the upload area

**Image Guidelines:**
- Maximum file size: 10MB per image
- Maximum total request: 50MB
- Recommended resolution: 1920x1080 or lower
- Multiple images supported per journal

**Image Best Practices:**
- Use high-quality photos
- Compress large files before upload
- Add images relevant to your story
- Consider image order (first image is the thumbnail)

### Rich Text Editing

**Formatting Options:**
- **Bold text:** Use for emphasis
- **Italic text:** For subtle emphasis
- **Line breaks:** Press Enter for new paragraphs
- **Lists:** Create bullet or numbered lists (if available)

**Content Tips:**
- Write engaging, descriptive content
- Include personal experiences and emotions
- Add specific details (dates, times, people)
- Proofread before publishing

### Privacy Settings

**Public Journals:**
- ✅ Visible to all users in "Explore" section
- ✅ Searchable by title and location
- ✅ Can receive comments and reactions
- ✅ Appears on your public profile

**Private Journals:**
- 🔒 Only visible to you
- 🔒 Not searchable or discoverable
- 🔒 No comments or reactions allowed
- 🔒 Not shown on your public profile

**Changing Privacy:**
- Toggle the **"Public"** switch when creating or editing
- Changes take effect immediately

## 📝 Managing Your Journals

### Viewing Your Journals

**Access Your Journals:**
1. Click **"Dashboard"** or **"My Journals"** in navigation
2. View all your entries in chronological order

**Journal Display:**
- **Timeline View:** Beautiful alternating layout (desktop)
- **Card View:** Single column layout (mobile)
- **Filters:** Sort by date, location, or visibility

### Editing Journals

1. Open the journal you want to edit
2. Click **"Edit"** button (pencil icon)
3. Modify any field (title, content, images, location)
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

**Editable Elements:**
- Title
- Content
- Location
- Images (add or remove)
- Privacy setting (public/private)

**Note:** Edit history is not tracked; latest version is saved

### Deleting Journals

⚠️ **Warning:** Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone!

**To Delete:**
1. Open the journal
2. Click **"Delete"** button (trash icon)
3. Confirm deletion in the popup
4. Journal is permanently removed

**What Gets Deleted:**
- Journal entry
- All uploaded images
- All comments on the journal
- All reactions to the journal
- Location data

### Organizing Journals

**By Date:**
- Journals are automatically sorted by creation date
- Most recent appears first

**By Location:**
- View journals on interactive map
- Filter by country or city
- Group by geographic region

**Search Your Journals:**
- Use search bar to find specific entries
- Search by title, content, or location
- Filter by date range

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

**Content Tips:**
1. **Start with a Hook:** Grab readers' attention in the first sentence
2. **Tell a Story:** Share experiences, not just facts
3. **Add Details:** Include sights, sounds, smells, feelings
4. **Use Quality Images:** High-resolution, well-composed photos
5. **Tag Locations Accurately:** Helps others discover your content

**Writing Style:**
- Write in first person (I, we)
- Use descriptive language
- Break content into paragraphs
- Include timestamps or dates
- Mention people, places, activities

### Photography Tips

**Best Practices:**
1. **Composition:** Follow rule of thirds
2. **Lighting:** Natural light is best
3. **Resolution:** At least 1080p for quality
4. **Variety:** Mix wide shots, details, people
5. **Editing:** Light editing for best presentation

**What to Photograph:**
- Iconic landmarks and views
- Local culture and people (with permission)
- Food and dining experiences
- Accommodations and transportation
- Unique moments and experiences

### Building Your Audience

**Engagement Strategies:**

5. **Be Authentic:** Personal stories resonate more

**Community Participation:**

## ❓ FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

### Account & Login

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the email instructions.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes, in your profile settings. Verify the new email before it's updated.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact support at support@odysseyjournal.com for account deletion.

### Journals & Content

**Q: How many journals can I create?**
A: Unlimited! Create as many journals as you want.

**Q: Can I schedule journals to publish later?**
A: Currently, journals publish immediately. Scheduling feature coming soon.

**Q: What happens to comments if I make a journal private?**
A: Existing comments remain but are hidden. They reappear if you make it public again.

**Q: Can I export my journals?**
A: Contact support for data export options.

### Images & Media

**Q: How many images can I upload per journal?**
A: No strict limit, but keep the total request under 50MB.

**Q: Can I upload videos?**
A: Currently, only images are supported. Video support coming soon.

**Q: What if my image upload fails?**
A: Check file size (<10MB), format (JPG/PNG), and internet connection.

### Technical Issues

**Q: The site is slow. What should I do?**
A: Try refreshing the page, clearing cache, or checking your internet connection.

**Q: Location autocomplete isn't working. Why?**
A: Type at least 3 characters and wait a moment for suggestions to load.

**Q: I see an error message. What does it mean?**
A: Note the error and contact support with details of what you were doing.

### Privacy & Security

**Q: Who can see my private journals?**
A: Only you. Private journals are completely hidden from all other users.

**Q: Can I control who comments on my journals?**
A: Public journals allow all users to comment. Private journals have no comments.

**Q: Is my personal information safe?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures.

### Features & Functionality

**Q: Can I collaborate with others on a journal?**
A: Currently, journals have a single author. Collaboration features coming soon.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: The website is fully responsive and works great on mobile browsers.

**Q: Can I download my journals as PDF?**
A: Export functionality is in development. Contact support for manual exports.
